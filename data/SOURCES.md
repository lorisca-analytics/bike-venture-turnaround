## Reading notes

- The report PDFs are image-based (no text layer). Numbers were extracted
  by reading rendered pages directly.
- "Market Share" report pages show results of the *previous* quarter
  (stated on the page). The Q4-dated report covers Q3; the Q7-dated report covers Q6.
  The CSVs are labeled by the quarter the numbers belong to, not the report filename.
